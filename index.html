How to Play

🎮

Move

Use WASD or Arrow Keys to move your character around the arena.

🌑

Survive

The shadow hunts you relentlessly. The longer you survive, the faster it gets.

🏛️

Obstacles

Use walls and pillars to break line-of-sight with the shadow.

⚡

Power-Ups

Collect glowing orbs for speed boosts, freezes, shields, and teleports.

Power-Up Guide

Speed Boost — You move 50% faster for 4s
Freeze — Shadow is frozen for 3s
Shield — One hit protection for 5s
Teleport — Jump to a random safe spot
